# SURVEY INSTRUMENTS

**Study Title:** Enhancing Trust in AI-Generated Financial Risk Summaries through Human-in-the-Loop Feedback
**Version: 5.0 | Date: 5 April 2026**

**Instruction on all questionnaire pages:** "Enter your participant ID only (e.g., P01). Do not enter your name."

## Survey 1: Demographics (pre-task, ~5 minutes)

- Current role/status (professional analyst / MSc-MBA student / CFA L2-L3 / final-year undergraduate / other).
- Years of experience analyzing corporate financial disclosures.
- Familiarity with SEC 10-K or equivalent filings.
- Prior use of AI tools for financial analysis.
- Optional: Age range.
- Optional: Gender.
- Optional: Industry focus.

## Survey 2: Post-task questionnaire (after each of 2 tasks, ~2 minutes each)

Embedded within the study system after each task. All items are required unless noted.

1. **Completeness** (5-point scale: 1 = Very incomplete, 5 = Very complete)
   "How complete was this summary?"

2. **Accuracy** (5-point scale: 1 = Very inaccurate, 5 = Very accurate)
   "How accurate was this summary based on the retrieved documents?"

3. **Citation helpfulness** (Yes / Partly / No)
   "Were the source citations helpful for verifying the summary?"

4. **Perceived control** (5-point scale: 1 = No control, 5 = Full control)
   "How much control did you have over the final summary?"

5. **Feature usefulness** (5-point scale: 1 = Not helpful, 5 = Very helpful)
   "How helpful was the feedback tool for improving the summary?"

6. **Open feedback** (Free text, optional)
   "Any concerns or observations about this task?"

**Notes:**
- Items 1-4 and 6 are shown in all conditions (Baseline and HITL-Full).
- Item 5 (feature usefulness) is shown in all conditions to enable direct comparison.
- Perceived control is expected to cluster at 1-2 for Baseline, providing within-subjects contrast.

## Survey 3: Post-session reflection and interview

Conducted verbally by the researcher after both tasks are complete:
- Mode preference (Baseline vs HITL-Full) and reasoning.
- Trust comparison between conditions.
- Perceived usefulness of chunk selection and summary editing.
- Suggestions for improvement.
- Semi-structured interview (audio recorded, 5-10 minutes).

## Changes from v4

- Removed SUS (System Usability Scale) and NASA-TLX instruments as these are better suited to larger quantitative studies.
- Removed 8-item trust questionnaire; trust is now captured through perceived control rating and qualitative interview.
- Replaced 7-point Likert agreement items with 5-point task-specific scales for completeness, accuracy, control, and usefulness.
- Added citation helpfulness item (Yes/Partly/No).
- Added open feedback text field per task.

## Estimated questionnaire time

Approximately 8-10 minutes total (2 minutes per task questionnaire + 5-10 minutes post-session reflection and interview).
