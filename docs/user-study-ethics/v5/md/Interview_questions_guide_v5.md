# SEMI-STRUCTURED INTERVIEW GUIDE

**Study Title:** Enhancing Trust in AI-Generated Financial Risk Summaries through Human-in-the-Loop Feedback
**Version: 5.0 | Date: 5 April 2026**

## Overview

**Timing:** End of session, about 10-15 minutes.
**Recording:** Audio recording only (participant face video is not routinely recorded).
**Setting:** Online only (Microsoft Teams).

## Pre-interview checklist

- Confirm participant is comfortable to continue.
- Remind participant of audio recording and right to pause/withdraw.
- Confirm participant is in a private space where they cannot be overheard.
- Record participant condition order: Baseline first or HITL-Full first.

## Core questions

1. Which condition did you prefer overall -- Baseline or HITL-Full -- and why?
2. How did your trust in the system's output differ between the two conditions?
3. How useful was chunk selection (choosing which retrieved sections to include) in the HITL-Full condition?
4. How useful was summary editing and hallucination flagging in the HITL-Full condition?
5. Did having control over the process change how you evaluated the final summary?
6. What improvements would you suggest for practical adoption of this kind of tool?

## Closing

Thank participant, invite final comments, and remind them of withdrawal route and data handling summary.
