# RESEARCH STUDY RISK ASSESSMENT

**Project:** Enhancing Trust in AI-Generated Financial Risk Summaries through Human-in-the-Loop Feedback
**Version: 5.0 | Date: 5 April 2026**

## Study format

One 30-40 minute session per participant, with two phases (Baseline and HITL-Full). All data collection is conducted online via Microsoft Teams only.

## Overall risk level

**LOW**

## Key risks and mitigations

**1. Privacy and confidentiality**
Use participant ID in all research datasets (e.g., P01-P08). Collect only screen recording and audio recording needed for analysis. Participant face video is not routinely recorded. Participants are instructed to join from a private space where they cannot be overheard. Store data on UH OneDrive with restricted access (researcher + supervisor). Store consent/contact details separately from research logs and questionnaire data.

**2. Data logging transparency risk**
Participants are informed that interaction logs include timestamps and task actions (query events, node selection/rejection, summary edits, questionnaire submission). No names are stored in interaction logs.

**3. Fatigue from screen-based tasks**
Breaks offered between tasks and on request. Participants can pause or stop without penalty and still receive compensation. The reduced session duration (30-40 minutes) further mitigates fatigue risk compared to the original protocol.

**4. Technical disruption**
Pilot testing before main sessions. Reschedule if severe technical issues occur. Full compensation remains payable.

## Age and consent safeguards

Participants must be 18+. Written informed consent is required before any data collection.

## Emergency and escalation

Any incident will be logged and reported to the supervisor and ethics committee as required.
