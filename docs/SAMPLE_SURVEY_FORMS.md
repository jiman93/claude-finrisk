# Sample Survey Forms: Ethics-Approved vs Proposed Design

**Study:** Enhancing Trust in AI-Generated Financial Risk Summaries through Human-in-the-Loop Feedback
**Date:** 2026-02-22

---

## Overview: Survey Timeline

```
Session Start (0 min)
    ↓
[PRE-TASK] Demographics Survey (5 min) ← EXTERNAL FORM (Google/Qualtrics)
    ↓
Tutorial with WMT (10 min) ← NO SURVEY
    ↓
Phase 1: Baseline (15 min)
    ↓
[PER-TASK] Post-Task Questionnaire #1 (2-3 min) ← IN SYSTEM
    ↓
Phase 2: HITL-R or HITL-G (20 min)
    ↓
[PER-TASK] Post-Task Questionnaire #2 (2-3 min) ← IN SYSTEM
    ↓
Phase 3: HITL-Full (20 min)
    ↓
[PER-TASK] Post-Task Questionnaire #3 (2-3 min) ← IN SYSTEM
    ↓
[POST-SESSION] Combined Survey (12-18 min) ← EXTERNAL FORM (Google/Qualtrics)
  - SUS (3 min)
  - NASA-TLX (3 min)
  - Trust Questionnaire (6 min)
  - [Optional] Retrospective questions (3 min)
    ↓
[OPTIONAL] Semi-structured interview (10-15 min)
    ↓
Session End (75-90 min)
```

## Key Differences Summary

### What's Different Between Per-Task and Post-Session?

**Per-task questionnaire** (appears 3 times):
- **Level:** Task-specific ("How complete was **THIS** summary?")
- **Timing:** Immediate after task (fresh in memory)
- **Purpose:** Capture immediate reactions to specific ticker/mode combination
- **Examples:**
  - "I trust THIS summary" (task-specific)
  - "How helpful was the chunk selector for THIS task?" (task-specific)

**Post-session surveys** (appear 1 time):
- **Level:** System-level ("I trust THE SYSTEM overall")
- **Timing:** After all 3 tasks (aggregated impression)
- **Purpose:** Capture overall attitudes toward the entire system
- **Examples:**
  - "I trust the AI system" (general, across all tasks)
  - "The system is easy to use" (SUS, general usability)

### Is There Overlap?

**Yes, intentional overlap:**
- Per-task: "I trust THIS summary" (specific to AAPL, Baseline mode)
- Post-session: "I trust THE SYSTEM" (general, after seeing all 3 modes)

**This is standard research design:**
- Per-task measures capture **within-subjects variation** (how does trust change across modes?)
- Post-session measures capture **between-subjects attitudes** (overall trust in the system)
- Both are needed to answer your RQs

**Example analysis:**
- RQ1: "Does HITL increase trust?"
  - **Per-task trust:** Compare Phase 1 (baseline) vs Phase 3 (HITL-Full) for same participant
  - **Post-session trust:** Compare overall trust scores between Group A vs Group B
- RQ2: "Does HITL-R vs HITL-G differ?"
  - **Per-task feature usefulness:** Compare Phase 2 scores between Group A (HITL-R) vs Group B (HITL-G)
  - **Post-session interview:** "Which phase did you prefer?" (qualitative validation)
